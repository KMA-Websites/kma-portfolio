/ 07 — BEYOND THE SCREEN

DESIGNING WITH
INTENTION.

I approach design with a player-first mindset. I’m especially interested in psychology: what motivates a player, how they read feedback, and why a choice feels rewarding. When I evaluate a feature or mechanic, I focus on clarity and the overall experience—while staying honest about technical constraints.

My curiosity comes from a wide range of games: the exploration of Journey, the systems of Pokémon, the world-building of Horizon Zero Dawn, the playful readability of Super Mario Galaxy, and the tension of The Dark Pictures Anthology.

I’m currently developing my own game while expanding my knowledge of production workflows, documentation, and player-focused development. I was born in Spain and am now based in Germany, continuing to grow through study, practice, and hands-on collaboration. My goal is to contribute to exciting projects, work with talented teams, and create experiences players remember.
