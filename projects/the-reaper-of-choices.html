/ THE QUESTION

WHAT MAKES
CHOICE MATTER?

The Reaper of Choices puts you in the role of a Reaper — not the Reaper of Death, but the Reaper of Choice. Your task is to make decisions that humanity itself cannot handle: choices where there is no obvious right or wrong.

The game explores how we form our own truth through the choices we make, how perception shapes what we believe, and what happens when you have to live with the consequences. At its core, it’s a philosophical exploration of choice, morality, responsibility, and whether we can forgive ourselves for the decisions we make.

01

Explore what influences a choice and why we believe the decisions we make are the right ones.

02

Explore

Build the narrative and systems around ambiguity, perception, and the consequences of choosing.

03

Reflect
