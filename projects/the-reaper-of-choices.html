02 / INDEPENDENT PROJECT

THE REAPER
OF CHOICES.

An original game project exploring the relationship between exploration, reward, and player-led discovery.

ROLE / GAME DESIGNERFOCUS / SYSTEMS + PROTOTYPINGSTATUS / IN DEVELOPMENT
/ THE QUESTION

WHAT MAKES
CHOICE MATTER?

The Reaper of Choices is a space to explore how systems can make a player feel responsible for the shape of a run. I’m using the project to study pacing, consequence, and the balance between discovery and readable feedback.

Rather than beginning with a large feature list, I’m building small playable experiments that can answer one design question at a time. Each pass helps me understand what the player needs to know, what can remain mysterious, and where a reward feels earned.

01

Map

Break the experience into choices, loops, resources, and moments of tension.

02

Prototype

Build the smallest testable version of the system and put it in motion.

03

Learn

Use playtests and notes to refine the rules without losing the player’s agency.
